# PRODUCT.md — Duta Mall Banjarmasin

## What this is
Web platform for Duta Mall Banjarmasin (PT Govindo Utama / Govindo Group) — the largest shopping, entertainment, and lifestyle destination in South Kalimantan, integrated with FUGO Hotel & Suites (4-star, above the mall) and Cinema XXI / The Premiere. Slogan: "Gawi Sabumi Kawa Manuntung".

## Who it serves
- **Weekend shoppers & families** — find tenants, facilities, and weekend events fast.
- **Movie enthusiasts** — check real-time-feeling Cinema XXI / The Premiere showtimes with theater badges (Dolby Atmos, D-BOX, Premiere) without leaving the site.
- **Business travelers & tourists** — understand the hotel-over-mall integration and book direct (25 min from Syamsudin Noor airport).
- **Prospective tenants & partners** — read mall scale, footfall signals, and official leasing contacts.

## What must be true on every surface
- Operates on real facts only: hours (10:00–22:00 WITA), hotline (0511) 327-8888, address Jl. Ahmad Yani KM 2, 12 core facilities, 200+ tenants, 180 hotel rooms, ballroom 300 pax. Estimates are labeled as illustrations.
- Bahasa Indonesia first; wayfinding codes (C21, FGO, FNB, RET, PRK, INF) are part of the brand voice.
- Interactive: showtimes selectable, facility grid answers, tenant filters work, booking widget computes, events remindable.
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.1 AA — contrast, keyboard, reduced-motion respect.
- Mobile-first performance (85%+ of visitors are on phones on-site); fixed bottom quick bar under md.

## Brand commitments
- Visual world is pinned by DESIGN.md: stark Lotte World Tower extraction — black canvas, white plates, single red #F00808, League Gothic + SUIT + Space Mono, 0px corners, color-blocking depth, with an earned neo-brutalist layer (2px ink borders, hard-offset shadows, checker hover fills, red ticker).
- The Duta Mall logo (red script + hibiscus mark) is the only pictorial brand asset; hero/campaign photography may be added later when licensed artwork exists.

## Out of scope (phase 2 per PRD)
Live Cinema XXI API, real room reservations, interactive floor map, tenant detail pages, PWA packaging.
